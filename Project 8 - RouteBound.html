Exercise 8 — Branch & Bound

Every route is a lower bound
waiting to be beaten.

Branch and Bound never checks all (n−1)! tours. It keeps a queue of partial routes sorted by their cheapest possible completion, always expands the most promising one first, and throws away any branch whose bound is already worse than the best full tour found so far. Watch that queue, and the pruning, happen live below.

Why pruning matters

As N grows, brute force checks every one of (N−1)! possible tours. Branch and Bound only expands the nodes that could still beat the best tour found. Same random-seeded cost matrices, computed instantly in your browser.
