Exercise 7 — Backtracking

Watch a queen get placed,
then watch it get undone.

The N-Queens problem asks for N queens on an N×N board with none attacking another. The interesting part isn't the final answer — it's the trying and undoing: place a queen, hit a row, or a diagonal, and take it back. This board shows every one of those attempts, live, exactly as the recursive backtracking runs.
